[B0006] KEYCORP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 NET INCOME OF $537 MILLION,
[B0007] OR $0.51 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE; DILUTED EPS INCREASED 24% YEAR-OVER-YEAR
[B0008] Record revenue of $2.04 billion, up 8% year-over-year and 4% sequentially
[B0009] Net interest margin of 2.95%, up 20 basis points year-over-year and 6 basis points sequentially
[B0010] Deposit growth outpaced loan growth - average deposits increased $3.6 billion sequentially while average loans held for investment increased $1.5 billion
[B0011] Investment banking and debt placement fees up 27% sequentially
[B0012] Common Equity Tier 1 ratio of 11.1% (a) ; repurchased $320 million of common shares during the quarter
[B0013] Generated a return on tangible common equity (b) exceeding 15%
[B0014] CLEVELAND, October 20, 2026 - KeyCorp (NYSE: KEY) announced third quarter of 2026 net income from continuing operations attributable to Key common shareholders of $537 million, or $0.51 per diluted common share. For the second quarter of 2026, net income from continuing operations attributable to Key common shareholders was $472 million, or $0.44 per diluted common share. For the third quarter of 2025, KeyCorp reported net income from continuing operations attributable to Key common shareholders of $454 million, or $0.41 per diluted common share, or adjusted net income of $450 million, or $0.41 per diluted common share (b) . The third quarter of 2025 included a $4 million after-tax benefit related to the updated FDIC special assessment (c) .
[B0015] Comments from Chairman and CEO, Chris Gorman
[B0016] "Our third quarter results reflect the strength of Key's relationship-driven franchise, the disciplined execution of our strategy, and the momentum we are building across the business. We delivered record revenue and record pre-provision net revenue (b) , while generating a return on tangible common equity exceeding 15% (b) . These results reinforce our confidence in achieving and sustaining a return on tangible common equity of 15%+ by the end of 2027.
[B0017] Our performance this quarter demonstrates the power of our balanced business model. Average deposit balances increased $3.6 billion sequentially, supporting continued net interest margin expansion. Our capital markets team delivered strong results with investment banking and debt placement fees increasing 27% sequentially. Concurrently, we maintained our disciplined approach to risk management, evidenced by broad-based improvement in credit quality metrics, including lower net charge-offs and nonperforming assets.
[B0018] Client engagement remains strong, supported by a constructive economic environment and continued confidence among the businesses and communities we serve. We continue to see opportunities to deepen client relationships, capture additional share of wallet, and drive sustainable growth across the franchise. Key is well positioned to perform across a broad range of economic scenarios, supported by our solid capital levels, strong liquidity profile, and well-diversified business mix.
[B0019] The ongoing investments we have made in teammates and technology are translating into stronger performance today while concurrently positioning us for greater value in the future. Looking ahead, we remain focused on executing with discipline, capturing the significant organic growth opportunities across our franchise, and delivering significant value for all our stakeholders."

[B0052] Taxable-equivalent net interest income was $1.31 billion for the third quarter of 2026 and the net interest margin was 2.95%. Compared to the third quarter of 2025, net interest income increased by $115 million, and the net interest margin increased by 20 basis points. These increases reflect a reduction in deposit costs as a result of declining interest rates and proactive deposit beta management, the reinvestment of proceeds from maturing low-yielding investment securities and fixed-rate swaps into higher yielding investments, an expanding balance sheet, and a continued strategic shift towards higher-yielding commercial and industrial loans. These benefits were partially offset by the impact of lower interest rates on repricing earning assets.
[B0053] Compared to the second quarter of 2026, taxable-equivalent net interest income increased by $50 million, and the net interest margin increased by 6 basis points. These increases reflect growth in commercial and industrial loans and higher deposit balances, the reinvestment of proceeds from maturing low-yielding investment securities and fixed-rate swaps into higher-yielding investments, and the impact of higher interest rates on repricing earning assets. Additionally, net interest income benefited from one additional day in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the second quarter of 2026.
