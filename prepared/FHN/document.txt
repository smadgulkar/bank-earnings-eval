[B0006] First Horizon Corporation Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Announces Strategic Balance Sheet Changes to Support Earnings and Growth
[B0007] MEMPHIS, TN (October 15, 2026) – First Horizon Corporation (NYSE: FHN or “First Horizon”) today reported financial results for the third quarter of a net loss available to common shareholders of $(94) million or loss per share of $(0.20). These results reflect the impact of strategic actions to further align the balance sheet with the Company's growth priorities. During the quarter, the Company began evaluating the possibility of selling ~$4.5 billion of select lower yielding consumer mortgages and, in early October, entered into an agreement to sell these mortgages. This transaction will be accretive to earnings on a go forward basis and will provide balance sheet flexibility to support future loan growth. The transaction is expected to settle early in the fourth quarter.
[B0008] Excluding notable items, adjusted earnings per share for third quarter 2026 was $0.53, down $0.01 from second quarter 2026 and up $0.02 from third quarter 2025. Reported results include the impacts of notable items totaling $0.73 per share, or $347 million after tax, primarily related to the reclassification of targeted mortgage loans to held for sale in relation to the pending mortgage portfolio sale.
[B0009] CEO Commentary
[B0010] “This quarter’s results are a testament to our disciplined approach and clear strategic priorities,” said Bryan Jordan, Chairman, President and CEO of First Horizon Corporation. “Average client deposit balances increased $1.3 billion in the quarter, and we continue to focus on growing and maintaining full client relationships and deploying capital where it best serves long-term value creation. Looking ahead, we remain committed to executing our strategy and delivering sustainable growth and value for our clients, associates, and shareholders.”

[B0029] Safety & Soundness
[B0030] Capital
[B0031] CET1 ratio was 10.3%, a decrease from second quarter 2026. The decline in capital was driven by notable items and primarily reflects the impacts of the reclassification of certain mortgage loans to held for sale.
[B0032] Asset Quality
[B0033] Provision benefit was $25 million, versus expense of $15 million in second quarter 2026. Excluding the impact of the pending mortgage portfolio transaction, provision for credit losses expense was $20 million, up from $15 million in the prior quarter. Net charge-offs were $36 million or 22 basis points, up from $33 million or 20 basis points in the prior quarter. Nonperforming loans of $482 million decreased $49 million. The ALLL to loans ratio decreased 1 basis point to 1.08%, and the ACL to loans ratio increased 1 basis point to 1.25% due to reclassification of certain mortgage loans associated with the pending portfolio sale.
[B0034] Profitability
[B0035] Net Interest Income & NIM
[B0036] Net interest income (FTE) increased $7 million to $686 million and net interest margin of 3.48% decreased 1 basis point. NII increased primarily due to higher average loan yield of 5.73%, which increased 6 basis points in the quarter. The NIM decrease was driven by higher interest bearing deposit costs of 2.45%, a 12 basis points increase, associated with higher average brokered deposits and higher customer deposit costs.
[B0037] Fees & Expenses
[B0038] Noninterest income decreased $487 million to a loss of $276 million primarily due to the recognition of a loss associated with the reclassification of select mortgage loans to held for sale. Adjusted noninterest income of $197 million decreased $14 million, driven by decreased deferred compensation, partially offset by increases in fixed income, service charges and fees, brokerage, trust, and insurance, and card and digital banking fees.
[B0039] Noninterest expense of $529 million decreased $2 million, driven by decreased deferred compensation expenses, partially offset by increases in occupancy and equipment, and other noninterest expense related to new account promotions.
[B0040] Growth
[B0041] Loans & Leases
[B0042] Average loan and lease balances of $64.5 billion decreased $206 million from the prior quarter, while period-end balances were $59.8 billion, down $5.5 billion from second quarter 2026. Loans to mortgage companies (LMC) decreased by $891 million at period-end, and consumer real estate decreased by $4.6 billion driven by the reclassification of certain mortgage loans to held for sale related to the pending mortgage portfolio sale.
[B0043] Deposits
[B0044] Average deposits of $68.6 billion increased $1.8 billion from second quarter 2026. Period-end deposits of $67.7 billion decreased $372 million driven primarily by a decrease in brokered CDs. Average client deposit balances increased $1.3 billion in the quarter and average brokered deposit balances were up $500 million.

[B0745] GLOSSARY OF TERMS
[B0746] Common Equity Tier 1 Ratio: Ratio consisting of common equity adjusted for certain unrealized gains/(losses) on available-for-sale securities, less disallowed portions of goodwill, other intangibles, and deferred tax assets as well as certain other regulatory deductions divided by risk-weighted assets.
[B0747] Fully Taxable Equivalent (“FTE”): Reflects the amount of tax-exempt income adjusted to a level that would yield the same after-tax income had that income been subject to taxation.
[B0748] Tier 1 Capital Ratio: Ratio consisting of shareholders’ equity adjusted for certain unrealized gains/(losses) on available-for-sale securities, plus qualifying portions of noncontrolling interests, less disallowed portions of goodwill, other intangible assets, and deferred tax assets as well as certain other regulatory deductions divided by risk-weighted assets.
[B0749] Key Ratios
[B0750] Return on Average Assets: Ratio is annualized net income to average total assets.
[B0751] Return on Average Common Equity: Ratio is annualized net income available to common shareholders to average common equity.
[B0752] Return on Average Tangible Common Equity: Ratio is annualized net income available to common shareholders to average tangible common equity.
[B0753] Noninterest Income as a Percentage of Total Revenue: Ratio is noninterest income excluding securities gains/(losses) to total revenue - taxable equivalent excluding securities gains/(losses).
[B0754] Efficiency Ratio: Ratio is noninterest expense to total revenue - taxable equivalent excluding securities gains/(losses).
[B0755] Leverage Ratio: Ratio is tier 1 capital to average assets for leverage.
[B0756] Asset Quality - Consolidated Key Ratios
[B0757] Nonperforming loans and leases ("NPL") %: Ratio is nonaccruing loans and leases in the loan portfolio to total period-end loans and leases.
[B0758] Net charge-offs %: Ratio is annualized net charge-offs to total average loans and leases.
[B0759] Allowance / loans and leases: Ratio is allowance for loan and lease losses to total period-end loans and leases.
[B0760] Allowance / Nonperforming loans and leases: Ratio is allowance for loan and lease losses to nonperforming loans and leases in the loan portfolio.
[B0761] Operating Segments
[B0762] Commercial, Consumer, and Wealth segment : Offers financial products and services, including traditional lending and deposit taking, to commercial and consumer clients primarily in the southern U.S. and other selected markets. Commercial, Consumer & Wealth also consists of lines of business that deliver product offerings and services with niche industry knowledge including corporate & specialty finance, commercial real estate, equipment finance/leasing, energy, international banking, healthcare, and transportation and logistics. Additionally, Commercial, Consumer & Wealth provides investment, wealth management, financial planning, trust and asset management services for consumer clients as well as delivering treasury management solutions, loan syndications, and corporate banking services.
[B0763] Wholesale segment: Consists of lines of business that deliver product offerings and services with differentiated industry knowledge. Wholesale’s lines of business include mortgage warehouse lending, franchise finance, correspondent banking, and mortgage. Additionally, Wholesale has a line of business focused on fixed income securities sales, trading, underwriting, and strategies for institutional clients in the U.S. and abroad, as well as loan sales, portfolio advisory services, and derivative sales.
[B0764] Corporate segment: Consists primarily of corporate support functions including risk management, audit, accounting, finance, executive office, and corporate communications. Shared support services such as human resources, marketing, properties, technology, credit risk and bank operations are allocated to the activities of Commercial, Consumer & Wealth, Wholesale and Corporate. Additionally, the Corporate segment includes centralized management of capital and funding to support the business activities of the company including management of balance sheet funding, liquidity, and capital management and allocation. The Corporate segment also includes the revenue and expense associated with run-off businesses such as pre-2009 mortgage banking elements, run-off consumer and trust preferred loan portfolios, and other exited businesses.
