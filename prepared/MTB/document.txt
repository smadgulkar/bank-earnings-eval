[B0058] As reported on M&T's Form 8-K dated September 28, 2026, effective for the third quarter of 2026 M&T made certain changes and reclassifications to Other income categories presented in its Consolidated Statement of Income. [B0146] Effective January 1, 2026, the Company elected to prospectively measure its residential mortgage loan servicing right assets at fair value with changes in fair value reflected in mortgage banking revenues. As a result, amortization associated with residential mortgage loan servicing right assets previously recognized in other costs of operations before 2026 is no longer recorded. Instead, beginning in 2026 fair value changes in residential mortgage loan servicing right assets, inclusive of the realization of expected net servicing revenues over time, are included in mortgage banking revenues. On December 31, 2025, the Company began economically hedging the risk of fair value changes in these assets through the use of various interest rate derivative contracts, for which changes in fair value are also reflected in mortgage banking revenues.
[B0147] Noninterest income in the third quarter of 2026 increased $3 million from 2026's second quarter.
[B0148] • Trust and investment services income declined $5 million reflecting seasonal tax services fees earned by the wealth management business in the second quarter of 2026.
[B0149] • Mortgage banking revenues increased $25 million reflecting a rise in residential mortgage loan sub-servicing income. In June 2026, the Company began sub-servicing $62.9 billion of additional residential mortgage loans.
[B0150] • Capital markets and advisory revenues decreased $9 million reflecting a decline in loan syndication fees and lower revenues from interest rate swap transactions with commercial customers.
[B0151] • Other revenues from operations decreased $10 million reflecting a lower distribution from M&T's investment in BLG in the recent quarter.
[B0152] Noninterest income declined $9 million, or 1%, as compared with the third quarter of 2025.
[B0153] • Trust and investment services income rose $13 million reflecting higher corporate trust revenues from the Company's institutional services business and an increase in personal trust and investment services revenues from its wealth management businesses.
[B0154] • Mortgage banking revenues increased $5 million reflecting a rise in residential mortgage loan sub-servicing income, partially offset by the impact of the Company's fair value accounting election described above and lower gains on commercial mortgage loans originated for sale.
[B0155] • Other revenues from operations decreased $27 million reflecting a $28 million distribution of an earnout payment from the Company's 2023 sale of its Collective Investment Trust ("CIT") business and a $12 million gain on the sale of equipment leases each in the third quarter of 2025, partially offset by an $11 million higher distribution from M&T's investment in BLG in the recent quarter.
