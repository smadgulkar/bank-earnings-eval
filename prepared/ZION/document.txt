[B0034] CEO COMMENTARY
[B0035] Harris H. Simmons, Chairman and CEO of Zions Bancorporation, commented, “Third quarter earnings per share increased 20% to $1.78, up from $1.48 a year ago. The higher earnings were primarily a reflection of improved credit quality, as net loan charge-offs were $3 million, down from $56 million a year ago. Although an increased pace of loan payoffs, combined with a highly competitive market, produced flat loan growth compared to the second quarter, customer deposits grew nearly 10% annualized from second quarter levels.” Mr. Simmons continued, “Short- and medium-term interest rates began to rise in the last month of the quarter, and we expect that our moderately asset-sensitive balance sheet should produce stronger net interest income in a higher rate environment, while we're seeing continued improvement in noninterest income that we expect will continue into next year.” Mr. Simmons concluded, “We were pleased to complete the acquisition of Basis Multifamily Finance I, LLC, a subsidiary of Basis Investment Group, at the end of July. This multifamily finance platform becomes an important strategic advantage in serving our commercial real estate clients, particularly in the fast-growing western markets we serve.”

[B0066] Net interest income increased $12 million, or 2%, in the third quarter of 2026, compared with the prior year period. The increase was primarily driven by lower funding costs and changes in the balance sheet mix, including growth in average loans and leases, money market investments, and deposits, partially offset by a decline in investment securities. Deposit growth outpaced loan growth, reducing reliance on higher-cost borrowed funds and contributing to higher money market investment balances. While these factors contributed to higher net interest income, the net interest margin declined to 3.23% from 3.28% in the prior year period.
[B0067] The yield on average interest-earning assets, net of hedging activity, was 4.91% for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 5.16% in the prior year period, primarily reflecting lower interest rates. The net yield on average loans and leases declined 28 basis points to 5.63%, while the net yield on average investment securities decreased 9 basis points to 2.64%. The yield on average money market investments decreased 76 basis points to 3.91%, as these short-term assets repriced more quickly in the declining rate environment.
[B0068] The rate paid on total deposits and interest-bearing liabilities decreased to 1.75% for the third quarter of 2026 from 1.92% in the prior year period, reflecting lower interest rates and reduced short-term borrowings. The total cost of deposits declined to 1.59% from 1.67%, as the benefit of lower interest rates was partially offset by growth in higher-cost interest-bearing deposits, which reduced reliance on more costly short-term borrowings. Compared with the prior quarter, total cost of deposits increased from 1.48%, driven in part by a shift in deposit mix toward higher-cost interest-bearing deposits and an accounting presentation change that increased the total cost of deposits by approximately three basis points.
[B0069] Average interest-earning assets increased $2.6 billion, or 3%, compared with the prior year period, reflecting growth in average money market investments of $1.8 billion and average loans and leases of $1.7 billion, partially offset by a $786 million decline in average investment securities. The increase in money market investments primarily reflected excess liquidity from deposit growth that exceeded loan growth, after first reducing short-term borrowings.

[B0201] About Zions Bancorporation, N.A.
[B0202] Zions Bancorporation, N.A. is one of the nation's premier financial services companies with annual net revenue of $3.4 billion in 2025, and total assets of approximately $89 billion at December 31, 2025.
